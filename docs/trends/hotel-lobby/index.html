About the trend

"Hotel Lobby (Unc & Phew)" is the 2022 single by Quavo and Takeoff, and their performance in front of a bright orange wall and a single studio mic became one of the duo's most replayed clips. The AI trend recreates that shot with new people: two friends trading lines at the mic, with every hand move and head turn kept.

Most face swap apps paste a flat face over the clip. Genjutsu keeps the original motion, camera and timing and rebuilds both performers from your photos, so the lighting and the movement match the original.
