About the trend

STORM is the best-known Genjutsu trend: one calm figure in white stands still in the middle of a huge crowd in black suits, and on the beat drop everyone around them erupts. Creators recast the lead with themselves, and versions like the Thai "mafia boss's daughter" edit spread across TikTok, Reels and X.

Genjutsu keeps the original crowd, choreography and camera moves and rebuilds the lead from your photo. Add a second photo to put one face on the whole crowd.

How to make it

  1. Open this page. The STORM crowd scene and the prompt are already loaded. You don't need to find the video.
  2. Add your photo. One clear, front-facing, well-lit photo for the calm lead in white.
  3. Optional: add the crowd. Add a second photo and the whole suit crowd gets that face. Your friend, your boss, your ex.
  4. Click Generate. 480p is the default. You'll see the exact price first, and the video renders in a few minutes.
  5. Download and post. Download the MP4 and post it with the original sound.
