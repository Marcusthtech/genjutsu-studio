About the trend

Lil Nas X and Jack Harlow's "Industry Baby" (2021) came with a prison-themed video whose pink-and-teal shower dance became instantly recognizable. The AI trend recasts it: one lead who dances up front and sings into the prison phone, and a whole line of dancers behind.

This template takes up to 8 photos, so you can put a full team, a friend group or an office in it. Genjutsu keeps the original choreography, camera cuts and timing and rebuilds each person from the photos.

How to make it

  1. Open this page. The Industry Baby clip and the prompt are already loaded. You don't need to find the video or a CapCut template.
  2. Add the lead. The first photo is the lead: the one dancing up front and singing on the prison phone. Use a clear, front-facing photo.
  3. Add up to 7 dancers. Optional. Each extra photo replaces one dancer, from left to right. Dancers without a photo stay as they are.
  4. Click Generate. 480p is the default. You'll see the exact price first, and the video renders in a few minutes.
  5. Download and post. Download the MP4 and post it with "Industry Baby" as the sound.

Who goes where?

Photo 1 is the lead (front, on the prison phone). Photos 2 to 8 replace the dancers from left to right.

What photos work best?

Clear, front-facing, well-lit photos of one person each. Avoid group photos and heavy shadows. Only use photos of people who agreed to be in the video.
