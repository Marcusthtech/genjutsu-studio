Why not a subscription?

Most AI video apps sell a monthly plan with a pile of credits. You pay every month, the "up to 60 videos" assumes their cheapest model and shortest clip, and unused credits disappear. You usually want one video for one trend, so that's what you pay for here.

Optional packs

Making a few videos? Packs add a small bonus: $5 = 500 credits, $15 = 1,600 credits (+7%), $40 = 4,500 credits (+12%). 100 credits = $1. You never need one: "Just this video" is always an option at checkout.

Questions

Is there a subscription?

No. There is no monthly plan and nothing renews. You pay once for the video you are making.

Do I have to buy credits I won't use?

No. At checkout, pick "Just this video" and you pay exactly that video's price. Packs are optional, for people who plan to make several videos.

How is the price calculated?

Seconds of the source video × the per-second rate for the resolution you pick, plus a flat $1 per video. The exact price shows before you click Generate.

What if the video fails?

Failed or moderated generations are refunded automatically to your balance.

Why do some apps look cheaper?

Subscription apps advertise "up to" numbers based on their cheapest model and shortest clip, and you pay every month whether you use it or not. Here you pay only when you make a video, with Higgsfield Genjutsu, the model that keeps the original scene.
