Questions

What can I make on the Custom page?

Upload any MP4 between 4 and 30 seconds, add 1 to 8 reference photos and, optionally, a prompt. Motion transfer recasts the people in the clip; object swap replaces one person or object.

What prompt should I write?

Keep it short and concrete, e.g. "Replace both characters with the uploaded pictures" or "Replace the man in the red shirt with the person in the photo".

How much does it cost?

You pay per second of the source video (max 30 s). At 720p it's about 34 credits per second plus 100 per video (100 credits = $1). The exact price shows before you generate.
